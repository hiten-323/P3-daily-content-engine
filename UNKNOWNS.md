# Unknowns

Open questions the system cannot answer yet, with the cheapest planned probe.
This file is expected to shrink as data accumulates.

## Audience (resolve with ~30 days of insights data)

1. **Which posting window actually wins for this audience** — 08:00 carousel vs
   20:00 reel post are now tracked separately; the data will answer it.
2. **Which funnel objective converts followers fastest at IGNITION** —
   DISCOVERY vs FOLLOW vs COMMUNITY rotation is live; compare follows-per-reach
   by objective after 4 weeks.
3. **Does founder-story content outperform coffee-culture content** — 8 founder
   topics are in rotation; viral memory will surface the answer.

## Revenue (resolve after first attributed orders)

4. **Actual PPOI** — no Instagram-attributed orders recorded yet; first
   non-zero `ig_revenue` snapshot establishes the baseline.
5. **Whether the conversion-reel cadence (1-in-5 days) is too low or too high**

6. **LTV / repeat-purchase behavior** — requires months of returning_customers
   data; until then AOV is the only value proxy.

## Distribution (need product/platform decisions, not just data)

7. **Email capture** — FLR (follower→lead rate) is unmeasurable without a lead
   magnet + email list. Decision needed: add a coffee-guide opt-in on
   p3online.in? (Owner: founder.)
8. **Link-in-bio strategy** — a UTM-tagged bio link (or link tool) would upgrade
   attribution from day-level to click-level. Cheap, high value, not yet done.
9. **WhatsApp channel** — nurture templates exist in the engine, but their
   conversion contribution has never been measured.

## Content production

10. **Video rendering** — the engine outputs paste-ready Nano Banana Pro /
    Seedance prompts, but actual reel videos depend on the founder's workflow.
    Unknown: sustainable weekly video throughput. This is the biggest
    execution-quality variable in the whole system.
11. **Whether AI-generated visuals pass the realism bar with real viewers** —
    REALISM_RULES are enforced in prompts, but only comments/shares will tell.

## Process for resolving

Each unknown gets resolved by: (a) accumulated pipeline data, (b) a cheap
experiment, or (c) a founder decision. When resolved, the answer moves into
ASSUMPTIONS.md (if it's now load-bearing) or SUCCESS_METRICS.md (if it's now
a baseline), and is removed here.
