# Hero Videos — your prime-slot drop folder

Drop a founder-produced reel video (`.mp4` or `.mov`) here and the engine posts
it as the **22:00 IST Instagram Reel** automatically — taking the prime evening
slot instead of the auto-generated motion slideshow.

Why: talking-head / real-footage reels are the format that actually spreads.
The engine reserves the best slot for YOUR best content.

- Oldest unposted file is used first (one per evening).
- Posted files are tracked in `output/learning/hero_posted.json` and never reused.
- No hero video today → the engine posts its free motion reel as usual.

Nothing else to do — just drop the file and commit/push (or upload via GitHub).
